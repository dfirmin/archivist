# Business Travel Policy

Owner: Finance Operations.

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees who travel on company business.
Contractors follow the terms of their own agreements.

Employees must book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is
required for flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Any single
trip expected to cost more than $2,500 needs written approval from the employee's director before
booking.

Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of returning. Meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day.

Exceptions to these limits require approval from the CFO.
