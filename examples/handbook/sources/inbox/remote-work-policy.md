# Remote Work Policy

Owner: People Operations.

This policy applies to all employees. Contractors follow their agreements.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's agreement. Core
collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the team's home time zone. Working from another
country for more than 14 days requires approval from People Operations.

Exceptions require written approval from the department head.
