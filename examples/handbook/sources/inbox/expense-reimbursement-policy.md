# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses the company reimburses and how to claim them.

## Scope

All employees. Contractors are reimbursed under the terms of their contracts.

## Policy

1. Submit claims in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense.
2. Attach an itemized receipt for every expense over $25.
3. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $60 per day.
4. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
5. Claims over $1,000 need approval from the employee's director before submission.

## Exceptions

The CFO may approve exceptions in writing.
