# Case Handling Process

This subject area documents how support cases are worked, not a data product.

Framework: every case follows triage, investigation, resolution and confirmation.

Components: the triage queue, the investigation playbooks, and the customer confirmation survey.

Roles: triage agents assign priority; specialists investigate; team leads approve closures over
30 days old.

Related subject areas: Customer Care (the data about these cases).
