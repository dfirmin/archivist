# Customer Care

The customer care subject area covers everything about customers contacting the company for help:
contacts, cases, and how cases move from open to closed.

Its data comes from Sample CRM, loaded nightly.

Related subject areas: Billing (for refund cases) and Customer Master (for customer identity).

Known issue: cases reopened after closure appear as new cases before March 2025.
