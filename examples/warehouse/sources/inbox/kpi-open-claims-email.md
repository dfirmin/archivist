all - since three of you asked, here's how the open claims KPI is counted for the monday deck

it's just a count of claims in CLAIM_HDR_SV where CLAIM_STATUS_CD is O or R (reopened counts as open), as of end of day sunday. one row per claim so no dedupe needed

we do NOT include claims reported in the last 24h because intake is still validating them

broken out by LOB_CD. that's it, no weighting
