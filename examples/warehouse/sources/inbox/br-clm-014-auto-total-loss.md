# BR-CLM-014 — Total Loss Determination

**Line of business:** Personal auto.

**Rule.** A vehicle is declared a total loss when the estimated repair cost is 75% or more of
its actual cash value (ACV) at the date of loss.

**Applies to:** all personal auto physical damage claims (collision and comprehensive).

**Effective:** 2024-01-01.

**Implementation.** Flagged in the claims platform; the flag lands in CLAIM_HDR_SV as
TOTAL_LOSS_IND = 'Y'.

**Rationale.** Aligns with the state threshold most of the book is written in and with salvage
recovery norms.
