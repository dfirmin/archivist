---
title: "Memo: change to the auto total-loss threshold (BR-CLM-014)"
---

To: Claims leadership
From: Auto Claims Product

Following the salvage review, the threshold in BR-CLM-014 (personal auto) drops from 75% to
70% of actual cash value. The change takes effect for losses on or after 2027-01-01; losses
before that date keep the 75% threshold.

No change to how the flag is stored.
