---
title: BR-CLM-031 salvage assignment (notes)
---

BR-CLM-031: once a vehicle is declared a total loss, salvage must be assigned to a salvage
vendor within 3 business days of the total-loss decision. Assignment is recorded in the claims
platform and shows as SALVAGE_ASSIGN_DT on the claim.

Effective 2025-06-01.
